The Problem

Technical B2B is where traditional agencies quietly fail

Complex products, long sales cycles, multiple markets and regulatory language: most agencies aren't built for it. Here's what usually goes wrong.

01

Your account lands on a junior's desk

The senior team wins the pitch. Then a junior account manager who has never spoken to an engineer runs your campaigns. The gap shows up in your cost per lead.

02

Nobody learns your product

Tender specifications, ISO requirements, OEM procurement cycles: if the person writing your ads can't hold a conversation about your industry, your audience notices instantly.

03

One playbook for every market

A campaign built for the UK gets machine-translated for France and Germany. Different markets buy differently. Localisation is strategy, not translation.

04

Reporting theatre instead of pipeline

Impressions, clicks and colourful dashboards, but the only question that matters goes unanswered: how many qualified conversations did marketing create this month?

FAQ

Honest answers to fair questions

Who actually does the work?

Strategy, media buying, optimisation and reporting: me, personally. Production work, design, video, development, native-language copy, is delivered in collaboration with a curated network of freelance specialists I've worked with for years. Every deliverable passes through my review before it reaches you. You always have one point of contact and one person accountable: me.

Do you have capacity for a company of our size?

Yes, precisely because I keep the client roster deliberately small. I take a limited number of retained engagements at a time, so every account gets senior-level attention. That's the point of the model: you get the focus a large agency reserves for its biggest client.

Why is there no pricing on this page?

Because a multi-market B2B engagement and a single-market campaign are different projects with different scopes. After a discovery call, you receive a written proposal with fixed, itemised pricing, no hourly ambiguity, no surprise invoices. Engagements are structured as a one-time setup phase plus a monthly retainer.

How fast can we start?

Discovery call this week, proposal within five business days of it, and campaigns live approximately four weeks after signature, the timeline in the process section above is contractual, not aspirational.

Who owns the ad accounts and assets?

You do, 100%. All campaigns are built inside your own ad accounts, tracking lives in your Google Analytics, and every creative, page and dashboard is your property from day one. If we ever part ways, everything keeps working without me.

How do you report?

A live Looker Studio dashboard showing spend, leads and cost per qualified lead per market, updated daily, plus a monthly written review: what happened, why, and what changes next month. No vanity metrics.
