Hi, how much is the licence with you? And can I pay in instalments?

Good evening Yanis! Our theory and 20-hour driving package is €1,390, payable in four instalments with no fees. Everything starts with a one-hour initial assessment, which sets the number of hours planned. Shall I suggest Saturday at 10 am or Wednesday at 2 pm?

WhatsAppMrs Lefebvre08:10

Hello, my daughter is 15 and a half, can she start accompanied driving with you?

Reply prepared by Claude

Hello Madam, yes, accompanied driving starts from age 15: theory, 20 hours of driving, then at least a year and 3,000 km with you as the accompanying adult. Shall I suggest a 20-minute appointment to present the programme and the price?

SMSSarah M.Yesterday

Hello, I couldn’t come in recent weeks, can I restart in December?

Hello Sarah, of course, you have 11 hours left on your package. Nadia has slots on Tuesday 1 December at 5 pm and on Thursday 3rd at 6 pm. Which one suits you?

EmailHugo D.Yesterday

Hello, I had planned to pay with my CPF but the site says I’m not eligible. Is that normal?

Reply prepared by Claude

Hello Hugo, yes: since 21 February 2026, the CPF only funds the car licence (permis B) for jobseekers or with co-funding, for example from your employer. We can offer you payment in four instalments, or a quote to send to your employer. Which do you prefer?
