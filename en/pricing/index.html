Frequently asked questions

Pricing and commitment.

Can I cancel?

Maintenance has no commitment. The Managed plan requires a three-month commitment, which gives enough time to measure the results.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay it directly to the platforms (Meta, Google) and it stays under your control.

What does the AI cost limit cover?

Maintenance includes AI usage costs up to a volume set during setup, tailored to your business. Beyond that, the price is adjusted with your agreement.
