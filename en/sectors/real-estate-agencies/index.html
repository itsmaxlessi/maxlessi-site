Frequently asked questions

The questions agencies ask.

Does the system work with my transaction software?

Hektor, Apimo, Modelo, Whise or SweepBright: depending on the software, we use the partner access, Zapier, or exports and contact emails. We check this during the assessment, before any commitment.

What about contacts from SeLoger, leboncoin or Bien’ici?

The property portals send each contact by email. The system reads it, replies within minutes with the property details, qualifies the project and offers a viewing. Your agent takes over whenever they wish.

Does the system do cold prospecting by phone or SMS?

No. Since 11 August 2026, cold calling a private individual without their prior consent is prohibited, and prospecting SMS also requires consent. The system works with people who have contacted you and with your clients, and keeps a record of their consent.

Who signs the mandates and the offers?

Always you. The system prepares the valuation report, the mandate or the viewing report. The agent reviews, approves and signs.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.
