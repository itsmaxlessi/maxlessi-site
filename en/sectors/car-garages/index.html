Frequently asked questions

The questions of garage owners.

Does the system work with my management software?

EBP MéCa, Fiducial, AutoProGestion, Kwixéo: depending on the software, we go through the open partner access, Zapier or exports. Your software remains the reference for quotes, repair orders and invoices. We check this during the assessment.

What happens when I miss a call?

The customer gets a text within a minute with your free slots. If they left a voicemail, the enquiry reaches your messages as a summary, ready to handle.

Are maintenance reminders allowed?

Yes, for your customers. Service, roadworthiness test or tyre reminders go to customers who have agreed to them, with STOP SMS in every message. The system never calls a private individual without their consent.

Do the quotes comply with the rules?

The system prepares the quote in your software with the mileage, prices incl. VAT and the reused-part option when it applies. You review it and send it.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.
