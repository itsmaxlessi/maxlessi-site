Frequently asked questions

The questions studios ask.

Does the system work with bsport?

bsport gives its partners access to its data. Depending on your situation, we use that access or a sync of the schedule and booking emails. We check this during the assessment, before any commitment.

What about clients who come through ClassPass or Urban Sports Club?

The system helps your team offer a direct deal to regulars who come through the platforms, in line with each platform’s terms.

Do my coaches need to use a new tool?

No. They keep using your usual schedule. The system works in the background, with your existing tools.

I have several studios. Is it suitable?

Yes. Each studio has its own figures, and the morning report compares them: fill rate, trials, follow-ups, cost per new client.

Who answers specific questions, such as pregnancy or an injury?

The assistant hands the conversation to your team as soon as a human answer is needed. It never gives medical advice.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT subscription?

With Claude, the Pro plan is enough to view your data and take action, on desktop and mobile alike. With ChatGPT, actions require a ChatGPT Business workspace, on the web. We advise you on the right plan during the assessment.

Can I cancel?

Maintenance has no commitment. The Managed plan requires a three-month commitment, which gives enough time to measure the results.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay it directly to the platforms (Meta, Google) and it stays under your control.
