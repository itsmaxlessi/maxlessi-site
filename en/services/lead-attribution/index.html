What if the customer calls instead of writing?

Calls from the website and from ads can be tracked; the rest is filled in with a simple question when the appointment is booked.

Do I need a CRM?

A simple CRM, or even a spreadsheet, is enough to start with. We set it up if needed.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay it directly to the platforms (Meta, Google) and it stays under your control.
