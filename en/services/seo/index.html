Frequently asked questions

In practice.

How long does it take to see results?

The Google Business Profile responds quickly; website pages usually take from several weeks to several months.

Does the website need to be rebuilt?

Rarely. We improve what already exists first.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.
