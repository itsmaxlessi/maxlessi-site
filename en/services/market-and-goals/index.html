Frequently asked questions

In practice.

What data do you rely on?

Keyword research tools, public ad libraries, public reviews, competitors’ websites. Every figure is sourced.

What if we don’t have any goals?

We set them together during setup, based on your current figures.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.
