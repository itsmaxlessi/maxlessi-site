Frequently asked questions

In practice.

Can the system make bank transfers?

No. We give it no payment function, and your bank requires your strong authentication for any transfer to a new beneficiary.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It prepares clean, reconciled data for them.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.
