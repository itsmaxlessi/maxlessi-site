Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Is it a chatbot?

No. The system connects your tools and automates your business: enquiries, CRM, appointments, follow-ups, advertising, reviews and reports. Replying to your clients is only one of its functions. You run it from Claude or ChatGPT.

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT subscription?

With Claude, the Pro plan is enough to view your data and take action, on desktop and mobile alike. With ChatGPT, actions require a ChatGPT Business workspace, on the web. We advise you on the right plan during the assessment.

Do I need to change my tools?

No. The system connects to your existing tools where they allow it. During the assessment, we check what can be connected and how.

Do your clients know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant introduces itself as an AI, in line with the EU AI Act. A member of your team can take over the conversation at any time.

Where is my data?

Your data stays in your accounts. A data processing agreement (GDPR) is signed with every client, and you receive the list of subprocessors.

Can I cancel?

Maintenance has no commitment. The Managed plan requires a three-month commitment, which gives enough time to measure the results.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay it directly to the platforms (Meta, Google) and it stays under your control.
